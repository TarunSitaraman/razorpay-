Where your money is stuck

Money that failed and is still recoverable, split across the four ways a payment can leak. One customer can appear in more than one.

Was the recovery worth doing?

Six strategies run on identical cases — same budget, same rules — only the decision of who to contact changes. Four are charted: the two that never contact anyone — an untouched holdout and retry-only — are the baseline the others are measured against. The winner is measured in net margin the merchant keeps, not gross cash that would have arrived anyway.

What we chose not to do

Every recovery tool reports what it did. Nearly none can say what it deliberately skipped, and why — which is exactly where a customer is protected from being chased pointlessly.

Guardrails in force

Checks that ran on every action. A block means the action was not sent; an escalation means a human approves it first.
